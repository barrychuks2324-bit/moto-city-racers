How do I steer?

Use the ◀ ▶ buttons, or choose "Tilt the phone" in Settings. With Ride assist set to Full, the bike follows the road for you and your steering changes lanes. Set Ride assist to Off when you want full control.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top right during a race. The game also pauses by itself if you lock your phone or switch apps.

There's no sound

Check the speaker button at the top right of the race screen, and make sure your phone isn't on silent. If the sound stops after locking your phone, tap the screen once and it comes back.

How do I earn coins?

Finish races. First place pays the most, and longer races pay more. For a 3-lap race:

1st place250 coins 2nd place160 coins 3rd place110 coins 4th place70 coins

Spend them in the Garage on faster bikes, paint and rider suits.

The game feels slow on my phone

Open Settings in the main menu and set Graphics to Low. The game also lowers its graphics on its own if it notices slow frames.

I lost my coins

Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
