Moto City Racers

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

In short: we don't collect personal data ourselves. Your name, coins and garage stay on your phone. The free version shows ads from Google, which collects some device data to show them.

Information stored on your device

Moto City Racers saves your rider name, settings, coins, garage items and best lap times on your own device. This information is not sent to us, and deleting the app removes it.

Online races

If you join an online race with friends, the game sends your rider name, your bike's colours and your position on the track to the game server so the other riders in your room can see you. This is kept in memory only while the room is open. It is not stored, sold or shared with anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your email address, contacts, precise location, photos or any account details. We do not run our own analytics.

Ads

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location based on your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data. On iPhone you choose whether apps may track you, and in the EU and UK you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy for partner apps. Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads between races.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads and coin packs are handled by the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that the purchase went through so it can unlock what you bought.

Motion sensors

If you choose tilt steering, the game reads your phone's motion sensor only to steer the bike. This data never leaves your device.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version uses more information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
